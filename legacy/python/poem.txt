Why not me?

I can see beauty in a lot of things,
I romanticize the smallest moments,
I try to make it all mean something,

I see beauty in the sky,
the land,
and the seas.

I see beauty in him,
in her,
in you.

Even in the feared ones:
moths,
crows,
spiders...

So if I see beauty in everything,
why is it so hard
to just find it in the mirror?
